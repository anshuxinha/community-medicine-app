OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
RCH add-on campaigns and missions that are easy to miss inside RMNCAH+N: Social Awareness and Actions to Neutralize Pneumonia Successfully (SAANS), National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission, Intensified Diarrhoea Control Fortnight (IDCF), and gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) under maternal health.

DEFINITIONS
SAANS: A Ministry of Health and Family Welfare campaign to reduce childhood pneumonia deaths by improving recognition, home care, and referral, aligned with social and behaviour change communication.
NATIONAL SICKLE CELL ANAEMIA ELIMINATION MISSION: A 2023 mission to eliminate sickle cell disease as a public-health problem, with a 2047 horizon, through screening, counselling, and care in affected tribal and other high-prevalence areas.
IDCF: An annual intensified fortnight (plus related Stop Diarrhoea campaign activity) for ORS, zinc, hygiene, and diarrhoea treatment at community level.

[SN]SAANS[/SN]

SAANS
- Pneumonia remains a leading killer of under-five children. SAANS pushes early recognition of fast breathing and chest indrawing, home care with continued feeding, and referral.
- Links: Integrated Management of Neonatal and Childhood Illness, Universal Immunization Programme (pneumococcal, Hib-containing pentavalent, measles), and air-pollution/household fuel counselling.
- ASHA and ANM are the field face. Do not treat SAANS as a separate vertical directorate.

[SN]National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission[/SN]

SICKLE CELL MISSION
- Launched 2023. Horizon taught: 2047. Focus: tribal districts and other high-prevalence pockets.
- Pillars: universal screening of eligible age groups in mission districts (including antenatal and newborn where operational), confirmatory testing, genetic counselling, hydroxyurea and pneumococcal vaccination as clinically indicated, patient support cards.
- Prenatal diagnosis links to the Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques Act (sex selection remains prohibited; diagnostic use for haemoglobinopathy follows the Act).
- Point-of-care solubility or HPLC algorithms vary by State; do not invent a single national cut-off.

[SN]IDCF and Stop Diarrhoea[/SN]

IDCF
- Annual intensification: ORS-zinc corners, demonstration of ORS mixing, handwashing, rotavirus vaccine already in UIP, sanitation linkage to Swachh Bharat.
- Home Available Fluids and continued breastfeeding remain the IMNCI lines.

[SN]Gestational diabetes mellitus in the programme[/SN]

GDM
- Diabetes in pregnancy that is first recognised during pregnancy. Screening in ANC as per national protocol (typically non-fasting 2-hour 75 g glucose, with programme cut-offs as in the current maternal-health guideline).
- Management: medical nutrition therapy first; metformin or insulin as per obstetric protocol; postnatal testing because many women convert to type 2 diabetes.
- Link NP-NCD and RMNCAH+N, not a stand-alone GDM directorate.

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- All four sit inside RMNCAH+N or NHM. Name the parent when the question is "national programmes for child health."

> **EXAM TIP:** SAANS = pneumonia behaviour change. Sickle cell = 2023 mission, 2047, tribal screening. IDCF = ORS-zinc fortnight. GDM = ANC 75 g test plus postnatal follow-up.
