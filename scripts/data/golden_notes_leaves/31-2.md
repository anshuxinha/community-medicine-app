OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
The Sample Registration System (SRS) is the Office of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner, India's (ORGI) large-scale demographic survey. It supplies annual national and State estimates of fertility and mortality. Figures below are from SRS Bulletin 2024 (Volume 59-I) and the SRS Maternal Mortality Bulletin 2022-24.

DEFINITIONS
SAMPLE REGISTRATION SYSTEM (SRS): A dual-record demographic survey that continuously enumerates births and deaths in a sample of villages and urban blocks, then matches that count with an independent half-yearly retrospective survey.
CRUDE BIRTH RATE (CBR): Live births in a year per 1,000 mid-year population.
CRUDE DEATH RATE (CDR): Deaths in a year per 1,000 mid-year population.
INFANT MORTALITY RATE (IMR): Infant deaths (under 1 year) in a year per 1,000 live births in that year.
MATERNAL MORTALITY RATIO (MMR): Maternal deaths per 100,000 live births in a defined period.
TOTAL FERTILITY RATE (TFR): Average number of children a woman would have during her reproductive years if current age-specific fertility rates applied.

HOW SRS WORKS
- Dual record: (1) continuous enumeration of vital events by a resident part-time enumerator; (2) independent six-monthly retrospective survey by a full-time supervisor.
- Unmatched and partially matched events are re-verified in the field. Verbal autopsy forms are filled for finalised deaths.
- Baseline survey of sample units records the usual resident population. The sample is replaced about every 10 years on the latest census frame.
- "Bigger States/Union Territories" in the bulletin are those with Census 2011 population above 10 million.

- Highest CBR: Bihar 26.8. Lowest: Kerala 11.1.
- Highest CDR: Chhattisgarh 8.4. Lowest: Delhi 4.5.

SRS STATISTICAL REPORT 2024 (ADDITIONAL)
- TFR 1.9 (rural 2.1, urban 1.5). Below replacement (2.1) for the fifth consecutive year.
- Under-5 mortality rate (U5MR) 28 per 1,000 live births.
- Neonatal mortality rate (NMR) 18 per 1,000 live births (NFHS-6 media brochure citing SRS 2024).
- Only six States remain above replacement TFR: Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh.

MMR BULLETIN 2022-24
- India MMR 87 per 100,000 live births (95% CI 78-95), down from 88 in 2021-23.
- Maternal mortality rate 5 per 100,000 women of reproductive age. Lifetime risk 0.18 percent.
- EAG States plus Assam: MMR 116. Southern States: 41. Other States: 75.
- Highest among tabulated States: Uttar Pradesh 154; Madhya Pradesh 135. Lowest: Kerala 24; Tamil Nadu 25.
