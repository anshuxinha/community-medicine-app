NUTRITIONAL CLASSIFICATION
- Weight-for-age and weight-for-height Z scores: if WHZ between -3 and -2, or MUAC 11.5-12.5 cm without oedema: moderate acute malnutrition. If WHZ below -3, or MUAC below 11.5 cm, or oedema: severe acute malnutrition.
- Marasmus: wasting, old-man face, appetite often preserved. Kwashiorkor: oedema, moon face, hair and skin changes, miserable, fatty liver teaching. Mixed: marasmic-kwashiorkor.
- NRC criteria if SAM with complications or failed appetite test. Uncomplicated SAM: community-based with energy-dense food as per State protocol.

DIARRHOEA (IF PRESENT)
- Duration, frequency, blood/mucus, fever, urine output, feeding. Classify: no dehydration / some / severe (IMNCI). ORS, zinc 14 days, continued feeding, handwashing, rotavirus already in UIP. IDCF messages.

PNEUMONIA (IF PRESENT)
- Fast breathing cut-offs by age (IMNCI). Chest indrawing: severe. Danger signs: not able to drink, convulsions, lethargy, stridor. SAANS: recognise, refer, treat. Amoxicillin as per IMNCI if pneumonia without danger signs at outpatient level.

COMMON PLAN
