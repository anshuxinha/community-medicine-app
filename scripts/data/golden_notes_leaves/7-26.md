OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (NMBA) is the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment community campaign for drug-demand reduction. It sits beside the National Mental Health Programme and Tele-MANAS, which treat dependence as a health condition.

DEFINITIONS
NASHA MUKT BHARAT ABHIYAAN: A 2020 community-based campaign of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment to reduce demand for drugs through awareness, volunteer networks, and linkage to de-addiction services.
DRUG DEPENDENCE: A cluster of physiological, behavioural, and cognitive phenomena in which the use of a substance takes on a much higher priority than other behaviours that once had greater value (WHO teaching definition).

[LAQ]Drug-demand reduction: NMBA and health-sector links[/LAQ]

NMBA
- Launch year 2020. Nodal ministry: Social Justice and Empowerment, not MoHFW.
- Components: master volunteers; school and college outreach; women and youth mobilisation; spiritual and community organisations; recovered persons as peer workers; toll-free helpline 14446.
- PIB at five years (November 2025): more than 23.74 crore persons sensitised; treatment-seeking reported to have risen several-fold; de-addiction facilities increased (490 to 780 in that statement). Quote the PIB date if you use those counts.
- Helpline 14446 is the NMBA line. Tele-MANAS 14416 is mental-health counselling. Do not merge the two numbers.

HEALTH-SECTOR LINKS
- Drug de-addiction centres, opioid substitution where available, and District Mental Health Programme handle clinical dependence.
- Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, frames dependence as a mental-health condition with rights to treatment.
- National Tobacco Control Programme and COTPA address smoked and smokeless tobacco (legal consumer products with a separate law). NMBA is illicit and harmful-use demand reduction.
- Alcohol: State excise and NMHP/Tele-MANAS; not the same as NMBA.

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- A complete answer names both ministries: Social Justice for the Abhiyaan, Health for clinical services and Tele-MANAS. Stigma reduction and treatment-seeking are the outcome, not arrest counts.

> **EXAM TIP:** Ministry of Social Justice, 2020, helpline 14446, volunteers and schools. Contrast 14416 Tele-MANAS. One line on MHC Act 2017. Do not write NMBA as an MoHFW vertical.
