OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
Drones (unmanned aircraft) move blood, vaccines, diagnostics, and medicines across terrain that delays road transport. India's i-Drone / ICMR and Ministry of Civil Aviation rules are the teaching pegs.

DEFINITIONS
MEDICAL DRONE DELIVERY: Transport of qualified medical cargo by unmanned aircraft under aviation and drug-safety rules.

[SN]Drones in healthcare logistics[/SN]

USES
- Last-mile blood units and diagnostic samples between blood banks and hard-to-reach CHCs.
- Vaccine and cold-chain-compatible payloads where roads flood or climb.
- Disaster: mapping, medicine drop, damage assessment.

RULES OF AN ANSWER
- Payload, temperature, chain of custody, and landing-site security matter as much as the aircraft.
- Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) traffic rules apply. Drug and blood rules still apply in the air.
- i-Drone (ICMR) is the named Indian research-to-operations effort for medical drones.
