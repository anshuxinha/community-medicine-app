OVERVIEW
Spotters in Community Medicine are identified objects, charts, or insects. State the name, one use, and one public-health link. This section is a viva card, not a photograph bank. Museum items remain the picture source.

PEDIGREE CHART
- Squares male, circles female; shaded affected; arrow proband; double bar consanguinity.
- Autosomal dominant: vertical, both sexes, male-to-male occurs.
- Autosomal recessive: skips generations, consanguinity helps.
- X-linked recessive: affected males, carrier females, no male-to-male.

MEDICAL CERTIFICATION OF CAUSE OF DEATH
- International form: Part I underlying sequence (a due to b due to c). Part II other contributing conditions.
- Underlying cause: the disease that initiated the train of events leading to death. This is what goes into vital statistics.
- Avoid writing "cardiorespiratory arrest" as the underlying cause.

VACCINE VIAL MONITOR AND SHAKE TEST
- VVM: heat-sensitive square on the vial. Use if the inner square is lighter than the outer circle. Discard if the square matches or is darker than the circle.
- Shake test: adsorbed vaccines (DPT, Td, Hep B, pentavalent, PCV). Compare sedimentation with a frozen-then-thawed control. If the test vial settles as fast as or faster than the frozen control, discard.

COLD CHAIN EQUIPMENT
- Ice-lined refrigerator (ILR): +2°C to +8°C for most UIP vaccines. Holdover time typically taught as about 20 hours at 43°C ambient for a functioning ILR.
- Deep freezer: -15°C to -25°C for ice packs and (where still stored frozen) OPV in older teaching; follow current UIP storage charts for each antigen.
- Vaccine carrier with conditioned ice packs for session sites.

GROWTH CHART
- WHO child growth standards. Weight-for-age is the usual under-five clinic chart in India.
- Plot the point; look at the channel (above -2 SD, between -2 and -3, below -3) and at the trend (crossing channels downward is faltering).
- A single low point needs confirmation; a falling curve needs action.

PARTOGRAPH
- Alert line and action line. Plot cervical dilatation against time in active labour.
- Crossing the action line: assess, augment or refer as per facility protocol.
- Also records membranes, moulding, contractions, fetal heart, liquor, pulse, blood pressure, urine.

ENTOMOLOGY QUICK ID
- Anopheles adult: spotted wings, palpi long in both sexes; rest at an angle. Malaria (and some filaria).
- Aedes adult: unspotted wings, black body with white stripes (lyre on thorax for Ae. aegypti). Dengue, chikungunya, Zika, yellow fever.
- Culex adult: unspotted wings, dull brown, palpi short in female. Bancroftian filariasis, Japanese encephalitis (Culex tritaeniorhynchus).

HORROCK'S APPARATUS
- Estimates bleaching-powder dose for a well. Cups that turn blue with starch-iodide have residual chlorine. First cup to stay blue gives the scoop count for 455 litres in the classic teaching.

CONTRACEPTION SPOTTERS
- CuT 380A: copper surface area 380 mm2. IUCD.
- Antara: medroxyprogesterone injection 3-monthly.
- Chhaya: centchroman (ormeloxifene) twice weekly then weekly.
- Mala-N: UIP/programme combined oral pill.

> **EXAM TIP:** Name → one identifying feature → one public-health sentence. For VVM say square versus circle. For MCCD say underlying cause, not arrest.
