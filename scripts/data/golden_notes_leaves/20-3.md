OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
Suicide is a public-health outcome, not only a clinical event. India reports suicide deaths through the National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) and addresses them through the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, the National Mental Health Programme, and Tele-MANAS.

DEFINITIONS
SUICIDE: A fatal act of self-harm with some evidence of intent to die.
SUICIDE ATTEMPT: A non-fatal act of self-harm with intent to die. Many attempts never reach NCRB files.
SUICIDE RATE: Suicide deaths per 100,000 population in a year.

EPIDEMIOLOGY
- NCRB Accidental Deaths and Suicides in India is the usual official series. Quote the latest NCRB year in the examination hall; do not invent a national rate here.
- Pattern taught across recent NCRB volumes: higher rates in some southern States; hanging and poisoning as leading means; family problems and illness as leading reported causes; students, daily-wage workers, and married persons appear as large absolute groups.
- Male rates exceed female rates in NCRB series. True burden is higher than registered deaths because of stigma and medico-legal under-reporting.

RISK GROUPS
- Mental illness (depression, alcohol dependence, psychosis).
- Recent interpersonal crisis, debt, crop failure, unemployment.
- Previous attempt (strongest clinical predictor).
- Adolescents and young adults; farmers in some States; LGBTQ persons facing violence and exclusion.

PREVENTION (PUBLIC HEALTH)
1. Means restriction: pesticide regulation, safe storage, barriers at known jump sites.
2. Care for mental illness: District Mental Health Programme, Ayushman Arogya Mandir mental health services, Tele-MANAS 14416 (24x7).
3. Decriminalisation: Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, Section 115, treats attempted suicide as a need for care, not a crime.
4. Media: avoid front-page method detail (WHO suicide-reporting guidance).
5. School and workplace gatekeeper training.
6. Alcohol policy (availability, brief intervention).

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- Tele-MANAS (10 October 2022) is the national 24x7 counselling arm. Full programme note is in 7-11. Link farmer distress to State mental-health helplines and debt relief, not to a single national suicide-prevention Act (there is none by that name).

> **EXAM TIP:** NCRB as source → one pattern line → six prevention headings → MHC Act 2017 decriminalisation → Tele-MANAS 14416. Do not write a fake all-India rate.
