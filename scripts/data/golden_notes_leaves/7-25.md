OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
Health-adjacent national schemes that appear as short notes: Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP), Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY), Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme (PMNDP), Pradhan Mantri Ayushman Bharat Health Infrastructure Mission (PM-ABHIM), Pradhan Mantri Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan (PM-JANMAN), and National AYUSH Mission (NAM).

DEFINITIONS
PMBJP: The Department of Pharmaceuticals scheme that sells generic medicines through dedicated kendras at substantially lower prices than branded equivalents.
PM-ABHIM: The 2021 health-infrastructure mission to close gaps in surveillance, critical care, and public health laboratories, including at the metropolitan and block levels.

[SN]Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana[/SN]

PMBJP
- Generic medicines, surgicals, and consumables through Janaushadhi Kendras. Price advantage versus branded products is the exam line (often taught as 50-80 percent cheaper; quote a current Bureau of Pharma PSU circular if a paper wants a single percent).
- Public-health point: access for outpatient care, reduced out-of-pocket spend, complement to PM-JAY (which is insurance for hospitalisation, not a pharmacy).

[SN]PMSSY and PM National Dialysis Programme[/SN]

PMSSY
- Corrects regional imbalance in tertiary care: new All India Institutes of Medical Sciences (AIIMS)-like institutes and upgradation of government medical colleges (super-speciality blocks).
- Not a primary-care scheme. Link shortage of specialists in some States.

PMNDP
- Free dialysis (haemodialysis) at district hospitals, often with public-private partnership. Peritoneal dialysis is being expanded in some States.
- Link NP-NCD / CKD and catastrophic expenditure.

[SN]PM-ABHIM[/SN]

PM-ABHIM
- Launched 2021. Building blocks taught: critical care blocks at district hospitals; integrated public health laboratories; metropolitan health surveillance units; health units at points of entry; block-level public health units; Ayushman Arogya Mandir infrastructure support.
- COVID-19 exposed gaps in surveillance and oxygen/critical care. PM-ABHIM is the capital mission that sits beside NHM's recurrent services.

[SN]PM-JANMAN and National AYUSH Mission[/SN]

PM-JANMAN
- Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs). Saturation of basic schemes: housing, water, education, health, connectivity. Health angle: mobile medical units, sickle-cell screening, immunization, TB, and nutrition in PVTG habitations. Link tribal health (chapter 13) and the sickle-cell mission (7-23).

NAM
- National AYUSH Mission: AYUSH services, educational institutions, and quality of drugs in the AYUSH sector. Co-location of AYUSH at primary facilities is the usual NHM linkage. Do not write that AYUSH replaces allopathic emergency care.

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- For a "schemes related to health" long answer: one table with scheme, ministry, one objective, one service point. PMBJP is Pharmaceuticals; NAM is AYUSH; the rest are Health and Family Welfare except PM-JANMAN (tribal affairs lead with health as one saturation item).

> **EXAM TIP:** Six rows. PMBJP = generics. PMSSY = AIIMS and GMC upgrade. PMNDP = free dialysis. PM-ABHIM = labs and critical care. JANMAN = PVTG saturation. NAM = AYUSH services.
