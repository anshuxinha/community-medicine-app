OVERVIEW
Worked postnatal (PNC) case. Puerperium is 6 weeks after delivery.

IDENTIFICATION
- Name: Meena. Age: 26. Day 6 after a term institutional vaginal delivery. Live female child, 2.8 kg. Nuclear family. Exclusive breastfeeding started within 1 hour.

MOTHER
- Loichia: decreasing, no foul smell. No fever. Fundus involuting. Perineum: episiotomy healthy. BP 120/80. Hb 10.4 g/dL. Passing urine and stools. Mood: tired but bonding; screen for postnatal depression with a few open questions.
- IFA continued 180 days as per anaemia protocol if still indicated. Contraception: lactational amenorrhoea is not enough alone if she will not be fully exclusive or if menses return; counsel PPIUCD, Antara, or condom.

NEWBORN (ON THE SAME SHEET)
- Cry at birth, no resuscitation. Cord dry. Urine and meconium passed. Breastfeeding: four signs of good attachment (more areola above than below, mouth wide open, lower lip out, chin touching breast). Position: head and body in line, close, facing breast, whole body supported.
- Warm chain: dried, skin-to-skin, delayed bath. BCG, Hep B birth dose, OPV-0 given before discharge (record dates).
- Danger signs (IMNCI): poor feeding, cold/hot, fast breathing, chest indrawing, convulsions, jaundice of palms/soles.

HOME VISITS
- HBNC schedule: ASHA visits as per current MoHFW calendar (day 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, 42 for institutional delivery is the usual taught set; confirm the card). HBYC later for 3-15 months.
- MAA programme: exclusive breastfeeding 6 months.

ENVIRONMENT AND ADVICE
- Handwashing before feeds. Safe water. No prelactals. Family planning, iron, diet, rest. Report bleeding, fever, foul lochia, calf pain.

> **EXAM TIP:** Day of puerperium → mother then baby → attachment four signs → vaccines given → HBNC visits → contraception. Do not skip the baby's immunization card.
