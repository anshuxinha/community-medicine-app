OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
Traveler's health is the prevention of illness in people who cross international borders, and the protection of India from imported infection. Yellow fever vaccination under the International Health Regulations (IHR) 2005 is the highest-yield single item.

DEFINITIONS
INTERNATIONAL CERTIFICATE OF VACCINATION OR PROPHYLAXIS: The IHR (2005) model certificate. For yellow fever it becomes valid 10 days after a WHO-approved vaccine given at an authorised centre, and then remains valid for the life of the person.
YELLOW FEVER ENDEMIC COUNTRY: A country on the World Health Organization list of areas with risk of yellow fever transmission. India is not endemic, but Aedes aegypti is present, so import control is strict.

YELLOW FEVER (IHR)
- Only vaccine that IHR currently allows States to require as a condition of entry.
- Dose: live attenuated 17D vaccine, 0.5 ml subcutaneous, at a Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) authorised centre in India (or equivalent abroad).
- Validity: from day 10 for life (WHO, July 2016 onwards). Older "10-year booster" teaching is outdated for certificate validity.
- India: travellers aged 9 months and above arriving from a yellow-fever risk country (or in transit outside the sterile airport area) must show the original certificate. Missing, photocopied, unstamped, or immature (less than 10 days) certificates lead to quarantine up to 6 days from last possible exposure, or until the certificate becomes valid, whichever is earlier.
- Booking in India: authorised centres listed by DGHS; digital booking exists on the government yellow-fever platform. Original paper/IHR-format certificate is what airport health officers check.

OTHER TRAVEL ITEMS
- Malaria: chemoprophylaxis as per destination (not for ordinary travel inside most of India). Bite prevention (nets, repellent) always.
- Polio: additional IPV/OPV for travel to or from polio-exporting countries as per current International Health Regulations temporary recommendations.
- Meningococcal vaccine for Hajj/Umrah as required by the host country.
- Routine: hepatitis A, typhoid, COVID-19 as per current advice. Adult immunization detail is in 32-4.

QUARANTINE VERSUS ISOLATION
- Isolation: ill or infected person. Quarantine: well person who was exposed. Yellow-fever arrivals without a valid certificate are quarantined, not isolated, unless they become ill.

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- Airport Health Organisation (APHO) and Port Health Organisation enforce IHR at points of entry. NCDC is the IHR National Focal Point. India treats the whole territory of a notifying country as infected when yellow fever is reported.

> **EXAM TIP:** IHR certificate: authorised centre, 10 days, lifelong, original only. India: 9 months+, 6-day quarantine. Isolation versus quarantine one line. Do not write a 10-year booster as current IHR validity.
