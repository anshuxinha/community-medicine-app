OVERVIEW
Arithmetic that appears in the practical: vaccine requirement, Horrock's bleaching-powder dose, and secondary attack rate.

VACCINE REQUIREMENT
- Doses needed = target population x coverage goal x number of doses in the schedule x wastage factor.
- Wastage factor = 100 / (100 - wastage percent). Example: 25 percent wastage → factor 1.33.
- Example: 200 infants, pentavalent 3 doses, 90 percent coverage, 10 percent wastage. Doses = 200 x 0.9 x 3 x (100/90) = 600. Vials of 10 doses = 60.

HORROCK'S
- Six cups. Each scoop of bleaching powder in the apparatus corresponds to a known dose for 455 litres in the classic teaching.
- First cup that remains blue with starch-iodide has satisfied chlorine demand. If cup 3 is the first blue, three scoopfuls per 455 L of that water.
- If no cup is blue, demand is very high (polluted). If all are blue, demand is already met by one scoop.
- Contact time: at least 30 minutes before drawing water. Residual chlorine target commonly taught: 0.5 mg/L at the tap after contact.

SECONDARY ATTACK RATE
- SAR = (number of exposed susceptibles who fall ill within the incubation range) / (exposed susceptibles) x 100.
- Exclude the primary (index) case from numerator and denominator.
- Example: household 6 people, 1 measles index, 4 remaining susceptibles, 3 fall ill → SAR = 3/4 = 75 percent.

> **EXAM TIP:** Write the formula with units. Wastage factor is 100/(100-w). Horrock: first blue cup. SAR: drop the primary case.
