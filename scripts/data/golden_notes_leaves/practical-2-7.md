OVERVIEW
Adolescent (10-19 years) and geriatric (60 years and above) cases share one sheet style: identification, problems, programmes, advice.

ADOLESCENT CASE (EXAMPLE)
- Name: Priya. Age: 16. Class 11. Menarche 13 years. Nuclear family.
- Problems to screen: anaemia (Hb, pallor, IFA under WIFS/RKSK), menstrual hygiene, substance use, mental health (sleep, mood, exam stress), nutrition, immunization (Td, MR if missed, HPV where the State offers it), sexual and reproductive knowledge without moralising, injury and road safety.
- RKSK: weekly IFA, peer educators, Adolescent Friendly Health Clinics. SABLA/Scheme for adolescent girls where applicable. Beti Bachao for the social frame.
- Advice: IFA weekly, diverse diet, no tobacco, helmet, 1098/Tele-MANAS 14416 if distressed. Consent and privacy: speak with the girl; involve parents for treatment.

GERIATRIC CASE (EXAMPLE)
- Name: Ramu. Age: 72. Lives with son's family. Widow. Hypertension 8 years. Cataract right eye. Reduced hearing.
- Examination: BP, pulse, vision (counting fingers), hearing (whisper), mobility, incontinence, cognition (orientation), depression, polypharmacy, fall history.
- NPHCE: geriatric clinic at DH/CHC, home-based care through primary team. NPHCE and Ayushman Arogya Mandir NCD follow-up. NPCBVI for cataract. Hearing: NPPCD. Social: Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007 (amended 2019); old-age pension.
- Advice: continue antihypertensives, salt, walk if safe, cataract surgery camp, remove loose mats, spectacles, family support. PM-JAY if eligible.

> **EXAM TIP:** Adolescent: RKSK plus WIFS plus mental health. Geriatric: 5 I's often taught (immobility, instability, incontinence, intellectual impairment, iatrogenesis) plus NPHCE and the 2007 Act.
