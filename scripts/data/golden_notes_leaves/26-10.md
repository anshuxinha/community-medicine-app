OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
This section collects exam-calculation methods that sit beside t-test and chi-square: Lot Quality Assurance Sampling (LQAS), design effect, survival analysis, Mantel-Haenszel, kappa, Bland-Altman, vaccine efficacy, and a short list of statistical fallacies.

DEFINITIONS
LOT QUALITY ASSURANCE SAMPLING (LQAS): A sampling method that classifies a supervision area (a "lot") as acceptable or not by comparing the number of failures in a small sample with a predetermined decision rule.
VACCINE EFFICACY: Percentage reduction in disease incidence in vaccinated compared with unvaccinated persons, usually from a trial: VE = (ARU - ARV) / ARU x 100, where ARU and ARV are attack rates in unvaccinated and vaccinated.
KAPPA: A chance-corrected measure of agreement between two raters for categorical ratings.
DESIGN EFFECT (DEFF): Ratio of the actual variance under the complex sample to the variance of a simple random sample of the same size. Sample size is multiplied by DEFF.

[SN]Lot Quality Assurance Sampling[/SN]

LQAS
- Used in immunization and programme supervision. A lot (block, PHC area) is classified, not a precise coverage percent for the whole district.
- Choose upper and lower thresholds (for example 80 percent and 50 percent), sample size n, and decision number d. If defectives exceed d, the lot fails.
- Fast, local, meant for action, not for a national coverage estimate.

[SN]Vaccine efficacy and effectiveness[/SN]

VACCINE EFFICACY
- Efficacy: trial, controlled conditions. Effectiveness: field, programme conditions. Efficiency: cost per outcome (health economics).
- Screening formula for coverage from efficacy is not required unless asked.
- Vaccine requirement for a campaign: target population x coverage goal x doses per person x wastage factor (1 / (1 - wastage fraction)).

[SN]Kappa, McNemar, Bland-Altman[/SN]

AGREEMENT
- Kappa: (Po - Pe) / (1 - Pe). Landis-like teaching bands: slight / fair / moderate / substantial / almost perfect. Do not treat kappa as a hypothesis test of association.
- McNemar: paired binary change (before-after on the same people).
- Bland-Altman: plot of difference versus mean for two measurement methods; look at bias and limits of agreement, not at a correlation coefficient alone.

SURVIVAL AND MANTEL-HAENSZEL
- Kaplan-Meier: nonparametric survival curve; censored observations contribute till the censor time. Log-rank compares curves.
- Cochran-Mantel-Haenszel: pooled odds ratio across strata (confounding control in a stratified 2x2).

DESIGN EFFECT AND FALACIES
- Cluster samples need DEFF > 1. Intra-cluster correlation raises DEFF.
- Fallacies: using mean for a badly skewed distribution without comment; treating non-significant p as "no difference"; multiple unplanned tests without saying so; correlating two methods and calling it agreement.

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- LQAS appears in immunization and NP-NCD supervision questions. Vaccine-requirement sums appear in UIP arithmetic. Kappa appears in reliability of a new screening tool.

> **EXAM TIP:** Write the formula asked (VE or kappa or requirement). One sentence on what the method classifies (lot vs population). Do not dump all eight methods unless the question is a list.
