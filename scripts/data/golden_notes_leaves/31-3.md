OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
The National Family Health Survey (NFHS) is India's large household survey on population, health, and nutrition. NFHS-6 (2023-24) was conducted by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) with the International Institute for Population Sciences (IIPS), Mumbai, as nodal agency. About 6.79 lakh households in 715 districts were covered. Compare NFHS-5 (2019-21) with NFHS-6 using the official India fact sheet and Press Information Bureau (PIB) releases.

DEFINITIONS
NATIONAL FAMILY HEALTH SURVEY (NFHS): A nationally representative household survey providing district-level estimates on fertility, family planning, maternal and child health, nutrition, and selected adult health indicators.
FULL IMMUNIZATION (CARD-BASED): Children aged 12-23 months who received BCG, three doses of DPT-containing vaccine, three doses of polio vaccine (excluding polio given at birth), and one dose of measles-containing vaccine, based on the vaccination card.

DESIGN
- Stewardship: MoHFW. Coordination: IIPS Mumbai.
- NFHS-5: about 6.1 lakh households, 707 districts. NFHS-6: about 6.79 lakh households, 715 districts.
- Biomarker and questionnaire modules change between rounds. NFHS-6 fact sheets dropped several NFHS-5 items (including anaemia prevalence, survey-based IMR/NMR/U5MR, and sex ratio at birth). Use SRS for those mortality rates.

Other NFHS-6 headlines (PIB / IIPS fact sheet):

INDIAN CONTEXT:
