OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
The National Health Policy (NHP) 2017 set time-bound goals for mortality, fertility, life expectancy, and health financing. This section places those goals beside the latest official estimates. Where a 2017 goal year has passed, the current figure is still the right exam closer.

DEFINITIONS
NATIONAL HEALTH POLICY 2017: The Government of India policy that shifted the frame from selective primary care toward comprehensive primary health care, and that published quantitative goals for mortality, fertility, and public financing.

WHAT MOVED THE OUTCOME GOALS
- Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, Child, Adolescent Health and Nutrition (RMNCAH+N), Janani Suraksha Yojana, Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram, and Ayushman Arogya Mandirs for births and child survival.
- National Tuberculosis Elimination Programme, National Vector Borne Disease Control, and Universal Immunization Programme for specific diseases.
- Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana for financial protection. Out-of-pocket share of total health expenditure has fallen versus the early 2010s (NHA), but remains substantial.

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- Financing is the lagging limb. Outcome goals that sit inside named national programmes moved faster than the 2.5 percent GDP spending goal.
