OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) is India's national digital health architecture. It creates a unique health identity, facility and professional registries, and a consent-based way to share electronic health records.

DEFINITIONS
AYUSHMAN BHARAT DIGITAL MISSION (ABDM): The national programme (formerly National Digital Health Mission) to build digital health public goods: identity, registries, and interoperable records, under the National Health Authority.
AYUSHMAN BHARAT HEALTH ACCOUNT (ABHA): A 14-digit unique health identifier that a person can create to link personal health records with consent.

[LAQ]ABDM building blocks and uses in public health[/LAQ]

BUILDING BLOCKS
1. ABHA number: unique ID. The person controls linking and consent.
2. Healthcare Professionals Registry (HPR): verified doctors, nurses, and allied professionals.
3. Health Facility Registry (HFR): public and private facilities.
4. Personal Health Record (PHR) / Health Information Exchange: discharge summaries, labs, prescriptions move with consent.
5. Unified Health Interface (UHI): open protocol so apps and providers can book and share without a single private monopoly.

PUBLIC HEALTH USES
- Continuity of care across Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, CHCs, and hospitals.
- Programme reporting (immunization U-WIN, NCD screening) can sit on the same identity.
- Deduplication of beneficiaries. Outbreak contact tracing is easier when facilities are in HFR.
- Telemedicine and e-sanjeevani become identifiable endpoints.

SAFEGUARDS
- Consent manager. Purpose limitation. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, is the privacy statute (see 29-13).
- ABHA is voluntary. Do not write that care is refused without ABHA.

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- National Health Authority implements ABDM. It is not the same as PM-JAY insurance, though both sit under Ayushman Bharat branding.
