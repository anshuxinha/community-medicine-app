OVERVIEW
LQAS decision rule, kappa, and a tiny vaccine-efficacy sum. Theory is in 26-10.

LQAS
- A lot (for example one PHC area) is classified as acceptable or not. It does not give a precise district coverage percent.
- Example teaching rule: n = 19. If 3 or more unvaccinated children in the sample, the lot fails a high coverage standard (exact d depends on the thresholds you were given). Always state n and d from the question. Do not invent a national n=19 as the only possible design.

KAPPA
- Two doctors grade 40 X-rays as TB present or absent.

|  | B positive | B negative |
|---|---|---|
| A positive | 16 | 4 |
| A negative | 6 | 14 |

- Observed agreement Po = (16+14)/40 = 0.75.
- Expected agreement Pe = [(20/40)*(22/40) + (20/40)*(18/40)] = (0.5*0.55 + 0.5*0.45) = 0.50.
- Kappa = (0.75-0.50)/(1-0.50) = 0.50. Moderate agreement in the usual teaching bands.

VACCINE EFFICACY
- Attack rate unvaccinated 80/200 = 0.40. Vaccinated 20/200 = 0.10.
- VE = (0.40-0.10)/0.40 x 100 = 75 percent.

> **EXAM TIP:** LQAS classifies a lot. Kappa chance-corrects agreement. VE uses attack rates, not odds ratios from a case-control unless the question gives those.
