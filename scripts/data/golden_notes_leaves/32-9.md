OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
Competency-Based Medical Education (CBME) is the National Medical Commission (NMC) undergraduate curriculum that organises learning around observable competencies rather than only hours of lectures. Attitude, Ethics and Communication (AETCOM) and the Family Adoption Programme (FAP) are named CBME strands that Community Medicine departments run.

DEFINITIONS
COMPETENCY-BASED MEDICAL EDUCATION (CBME): A curriculum that states what the graduate must be able to do, and that assesses those abilities, rather than counting only time spent in class.
AETCOM: Attitude, Ethics and Communication, the NMC modules that teach professional behaviour, ethics, and communication across MBBS phases.
FAMILY ADOPTION PROGRAMME (FAP): A CBME requirement in which each student is assigned families in the community for longitudinal follow-up of health, nutrition, and utilisation of services.

CBME IN COMMUNITY MEDICINE
- Competencies cover family study, clinico-social cases, epidemiology exercises, entomology, and national programmes.
- Teaching methods: small-group teaching, self-directed learning, clinical postings in the community (typically two postings), and integrated sessions with other departments.
- Assessment: internal assessment plus university theory and practical (spots, family or clinico-social case, viva, statistics exercises).

AETCOM
- Modules run through all professional years. Community Medicine often shares modules on doctor-patient communication, consent, equity, and professionalism.
- Link Mental Healthcare Act (capacity and consent), Consumer Protection, and the NMC code of conduct.

FAMILY ADOPTION PROGRAMME
- Each student follows allotted families over the MBBS years: household survey, antenatal and under-five follow-up, immunization, lifestyle risks, and referral.
- Logbook is mandatory. FAP hours sit in the NMC CBME time table (community visits plus logbook).
- Exam overlap with family-study practical: FAP is longitudinal and community-assigned; the university family case may be a different household on exam day.

RECENT NMC REFORMS (TEACHING LINES)
- Undergraduate CBME regulations (including the 2024 hours notification) change phase-wise teaching hours. Quote the circular year if the question asks for hours.
- Postgraduate eligibility still includes research methodology, ethics, and basic/advanced life-support courses in the first year (NMC PG regulations).
