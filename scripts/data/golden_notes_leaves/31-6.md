OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
India uses several complementary systems for population and health data. No single system answers every exam question. This section is a comparison table of Census, Sample Registration System (SRS), Civil Registration System (CRS), National Family Health Survey (NFHS), Health Management Information System (HMIS), and the Integrated Health Information Platform (IHIP).

DEFINITIONS
CIVIL REGISTRATION SYSTEM (CRS): Continuous, compulsory, permanent recording of vital events (live births, deaths, stillbirths) under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969 (as amended).
HEALTH MANAGEMENT INFORMATION SYSTEM (HMIS): Routine facility-based reporting of service delivery under the National Health Mission (NHM).
INTEGRATED HEALTH INFORMATION PLATFORM (IHIP): Web-based, case-based surveillance platform that succeeded the earlier Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (IDSP) portal for outbreak-prone diseases.

COMPARISON TABLE

| System | Since / cycle | What it gives | Strength | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Census | 1872; synchronous from 1881; every 10 years | Population by age, sex, literacy, density, housing | Complete count to village; sampling frame for other surveys | Decadal. 2021 round deferred; 2027 is the current operation |
| SRS | 1969-70 onwards; annual | CBR, CDR, IMR, TFR, NMR, U5MR, MMR (separate bulletin) | Dual-record; considered the standard for national fertility and mortality rates | Sample, not a complete count. MMR needs pooled years |
| CRS | Statutory from 1969; continuous | Registered births and deaths, cause of death where medical certification exists | Legal identity; district and city detail | Completeness still uneven across States. Medical certification of cause of death is incomplete |
| NFHS | About every 5 years (NFHS-6: 2023-24) | Fertility, ANC, institutional delivery, immunization, nutrition, selected adult risks | District estimates; biomarker modules | Not the official annual IMR. Modules change between rounds |
| HMIS | Continuous facility reporting | ANC, deliveries, immunization doses, programme output | Timely, programme-linked | Facility-based; quality and completeness vary |
| IHIP / IDSP | IDSP from 2004; IHIP case-based | Outbreak-prone disease reporting, S, P, L forms historically; case-based now | Early warning | Depends on reporting discipline at periphery |

OTHER SURVEYS (ONE-LINE EACH)
- District Level Household and Facility Survey (DLHS): older RCH facility and household rounds; largely superseded by NFHS and Health Facility surveys.
- National Sample Survey (NSS) health rounds: out-of-pocket expenditure, morbidity, hospitalisation.
- National Family Health Survey versus Comprehensive National Nutrition Survey (CNNS): CNNS is nutrition-biochemistry depth for children and adolescents (one major round).

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- Exam rule: mortality rates (IMR, MMR, TFR) from SRS; coverage (ANC, immunization, stunting) from NFHS; headcount from Census; legal vital events from CRS; weekly outbreaks from IHIP.

**Mnemonic:** C-S-C-N-H-I
- C: Census (count)
- S: SRS (rates)
- C: CRS (register)
- N: NFHS (coverage)
- H: HMIS (facility)
- I: IHIP (outbreak)
