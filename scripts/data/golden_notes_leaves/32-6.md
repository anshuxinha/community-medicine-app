OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is the ability of bacteria, viruses, fungi, or parasites to survive medicines that previously killed or inhibited them. India's second National Action Plan on Antimicrobial Resistance (NAP-AMR 2.0) covers 2025-2029. It follows NAP-AMR 2017-2021 and the World Health Organization (WHO) Global Action Plan on AMR.

DEFINITIONS
ANTIMICROBIAL RESISTANCE (AMR): The process by which microorganisms stop responding to antimicrobials, making infections harder to treat and raising the risk of spread, severe disease, and death.
ANTIMICROBIAL STEWARDSHIP: Coordinated interventions that promote the optimal use of antimicrobials: right drug, dose, duration, and route.
AWaRe: WHO grouping of antibiotics into Access, Watch, and Reserve, used to track and improve prescribing.

NAP-AMR 1.0 VERSUS 2.0
- 1.0 (2017-2021): broad One Health framework. Gaps: weak ownership outside health, thin private-sector engagement, limited budgets and timelines.
- 2.0 launched 18 November 2025 (World AMR Awareness Week) by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW). Period 2025-2029.
- More than 20 ministries (health, animal husbandry, agriculture, environment, Jal Shakti, pharmaceuticals, education, science) each have an action plan with goals, timelines, and budget.

SIX STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES (TEACHING LIST)
1. Awareness, education, and training.
2. Laboratory capacity and surveillance of AMR and antibiotic residues (human, animal, food, environment).
3. Infection prevention and control (IPC) to cut new infections.
4. Optimise antimicrobial use: access plus stewardship in humans, animals, and food.
5. Research and innovation (diagnostics, alternatives, operational research). India AMR Innovation Hub is the named innovation arm.
6. Intersectoral governance: coordination committee and technical advisory group hosted by MoHFW; NITI Aayog oversight for monitoring.

EXAM CONTRASTS
- Access versus excess: India must still deliver antibiotics to people who need them, while stopping over-the-counter and livestock misuse.
- AWaRe: increase the Access share of total antibiotic use; keep Reserve for last-line, specialist use.
- Biosafety versus biosecurity is a different pair (see 32-5). AMR is evolutionary pressure from use, not a bioterrorism event.

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- ICMR AMR surveillance network and NCDC National AMR programme supply human-sector data. Veterinary and farm use sit with Animal Husbandry. Hospital IPC and BMW rules cut transmission of resistant organisms.
