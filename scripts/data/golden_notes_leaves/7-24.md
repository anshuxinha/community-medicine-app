OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
Quality and sanitation initiatives that sit beside Swachh Bharat: Kayakalp awards for public facilities, Swachh Swasth Sarvatra, and VISHWAS (Village-based Initiative to Synergise Health, Water and Sanitation).

DEFINITIONS
KAYAKALP: A National Health Mission award scheme that scores public health facilities on cleanliness, hygiene, infection control, waste, and related quality, and that pays cash incentives to high scorers.
SWACHH SWASTH SARVATRA: A joint initiative linking Kayakalp-awarded facilities with Open Defecation Free (ODF) status of their service area, so sanitation in the hospital and sanitation in the village move together.

[LAQ]Kayakalp: criteria, assessment, and awards[/LAQ]

KAYAKALP
- Launched to extend Swachh Bharat into public hospitals. Internal assessment, peer assessment, then external assessment using a checklist.
- Eight criteria commonly taught: hospital upkeep; sanitation and hygiene; waste management; infection control; support services; hygiene promotion; cleanliness beyond the boundary wall; eco-friendly facility (added later).
- Awards: ranked winner and runner-up by level (district hospital, CHC/SDH, PHC, urban facilities, Health and Wellness Centre / Ayushman Arogya Mandir). Commendation certificate if the score crosses the published cut-off (often taught as 70 percent).
- Cash awards (illustrative national scale from MoHFW guidelines): district hospital winner about Rs 50 lakh; CHC winner about Rs 15 lakh; PHC winner about Rs 2 lakh per district. Quote the current year's State circular if the paper asks exact rupees.
- Use of money: typically a large share for facility improvement and a share as staff incentive.

SWACHH SWASTH SARVATRA
- Three-way linkage: a Kayakalp facility, ODF declared service area, and support to remaining villages to reach ODF. The slogan is that a clean hospital in a dirty village is incomplete.

VISHWAS
- Village-based Initiative to Synergise Health, Water and Sanitation. Community platform to join ASHA/VHSNC work with water and sanitation (Jal Jeevan Mission, Swachh Bharat). Useful one-liner in a sanitation-and-health viva.

INDIAN CONTEXT:
- Kayakalp is a quality-improvement cycle, not a one-time prize. Link biomedical waste rules and National Quality Assurance Standards (NQAS). NQAS is the broader quality certification; Kayakalp is the cleanliness-and-infection-control award.
