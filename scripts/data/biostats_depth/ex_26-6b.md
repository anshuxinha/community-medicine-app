[EX]63. Define standard error of a proportion. Eosinophils 10 percent in 200 white cells. What are the lowest and highest percentages expected on repeating the count?[/EX]
[ANS]
SE(p) = √(pq / n), the sampling SD of a sample proportion.

p = 0.10, n = 200, SE = √(0.09/200) = 0.0212
95 percent limits: 0.10 ± 1.96 × 0.0212 = **0.058 to 0.142**, that is **5.8 percent to 14.2 percent**.
[/ANS]
