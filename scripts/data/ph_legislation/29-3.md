OVERVIEW OF THE CHAPTER
This leaf is the women's statute cluster: paid maternity, civil protection from domestic violence, dowry, commercial sexual exploitation, and the short Sati Act. Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) and the Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques (Prohibition of Sex Selection) Act, 1994 (PCPNDT Act) are written in full in the reproductive-law leaf (29-1). They reappear here only as grouped headings when the long question is “legislation for women's empowerment and maternal health”. Employees' State Insurance maternity leave of twenty-six weeks is named in one line; the occupational-health leaf holds the Employees' State Insurance scheme.

DEFINITIONS
MATERNITY BENEFIT: Payment, at the rate of the average daily wage for the period of her actual absence, to a woman for the period immediately preceding and including the day of her delivery and for the period immediately following that day, as provided in the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961.

DOMESTIC VIOLENCE: For the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005, harm or injury to the health, safety, life, limb, or well-being of the aggrieved person, including physical, sexual, verbal and emotional, and economic abuse, or a threat of such abuse, by a respondent who is or has been in a domestic relationship with her.

DOWRY: Any property or valuable security given or agreed to be given, directly or indirectly, by one party to a marriage to the other party, or by the parents of either party, or by any other person, at, before, or any time after the marriage, in connection with the marriage, other than dower or mahr in the case of persons to whom the Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) applies.

[SN]Maternity Benefit Act[/SN]

MATERNITY BENEFIT ACT, 1961 (AMENDMENT 2017)
The Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (MBA) regulates employment of women in certain establishments for specified periods before and after child-birth and provides for maternity benefit and certain other benefits. The Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017, effective 1 April 2017, is the current teaching text. Classic application: establishments with ten or more employees. The same entitlements are mapped into the Code on Social Security, 2020. If the question names the Maternity Benefit Act, write the 1961 Act and the 2017 Amendment.

PAID LEAVE (LEARN THIS TABLE)

| Situation | Duration | Prenatal cap / start |
|---|---|---|
| First two surviving children (woman with fewer than two surviving children) | 26 weeks | Not more than 8 weeks preceding the expected date of delivery |
| Third and subsequent child (two or more surviving children) | 12 weeks | Not more than 6 weeks preceding the expected date of delivery, as provided |
| Adopting mother (child below three months of age) | 12 weeks | From the date the child is handed over |
| Commissioning mother (biological mother who uses her egg to create an embryo implanted in another woman) | 12 weeks | From the date the child is handed over |

OTHER 2017 PROVISIONS
- Creche: every establishment with fifty or more employees shall have the facility of a creche (section 11A). The woman is allowed four visits a day to the creche, including the interval for rest.
- Work from home: if the nature of work assigned so allows, the employer may permit the woman to work from home after availing maternity benefit, on such conditions as they may mutually agree.
- Nursing breaks and prohibition of dismissal during maternity absence remain from the parent Act.
- Medical bonus is payable where prenatal confinement and postnatal care are not provided free by the employer, as laid down.

NATIONAL CONTEXT
Twenty-six weeks aligns India with a longer paid leave than the earlier twelve-week rule and with International Labour Organization teaching on maternity protection. Pair MBA with antenatal care under the reproductive, maternal, newborn, child, and adolescent health programme, and with the Employees' State Insurance maternity benefit of twenty-six weeks (occupational-health leaf). The creche clause is the examiner's favourite “section number” after the 26-week figure.

> **EXAM TIP:** Effective 1 April 2017: 26 weeks for the first two children (not more than 8 weeks prenatally), 12 weeks thereafter, 12 weeks for adopting and commissioning mothers, creche at 50 employees (section 11A), work from home if the work allows, ten-or-more establishment rule.

[SN]Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act[/SN]

PROTECTION OF WOMEN FROM DOMESTIC VIOLENCE ACT, 2005
The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 (PWDVA) is a civil protection statute. It does not replace the Indian Penal Code (or its successor code) offences of hurt, cruelty, or sexual assault. It adds a magistrate's power to pass immediate protection, residence, monetary, and custody orders for an aggrieved woman in a domestic relationship.

DEFINITION OF ABUSE
Domestic violence includes:

1. Physical abuse.
2. Sexual abuse.
3. Verbal and emotional abuse.
4. Economic abuse.

It also includes threats of such abuse, and harassment for dowry. The woman need not be a currently married wife. A domestic relationship covers marriage, live-in relationships in the nature of marriage, and specified family relationships. She has a right to reside in the shared household, whether or not she has title.

**Mnemonic:** PS-VEE
- P: Physical
- S: Sexual
- VE: Verbal and emotional
- E: Economic

MACHINERY AND ORDERS
- Protection Officer: appointed by the State, receives the complaint, prepares a domestic-incident report, and helps the woman reach shelter, legal aid, and medical examination.
- Service providers and medical facilities: a medical facility shall provide medical aid when approached; it shall not demand any statutory form as a precondition of emergency care.
- Magistrate: may pass a protection order (no violence, no communication, no alienating assets), a residence order (no eviction from the shared household; optionally alternative accommodation), a monetary-relief order, a custody order for children, and a compensation order.

INDIA HOOK
For a Medical Officer the Act means: record injuries, treat first, and help the woman reach the Protection Officer. Do not send her home solely because she is “still married”. Shared household is a statutory right, not a courtesy of the in-laws.

> **EXAM TIP:** Call it a civil Act. Recite PS-VEE, shared household, Protection Officer, and the four magistrate orders (protection, residence, monetary, custody). Add compensation if you have time.

[SN]Dowry Prohibition Act[/SN]

DOWRY PROHIBITION ACT, 1961
The Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 makes giving, taking, or abetting the giving or taking of dowry an offence. Demand of dowry is also an offence. Presents of a customary nature, entered in a list as provided, are treated separately from dowry.

The 1986 Amendment provides a minimum punishment of five years and a fine of Rs 15,000 (or the amount of the dowry, whichever is more) for taking or abetting the taking of dowry. Dowry death and cruelty for dowry remain in the criminal code and are written in the forensic answer. Here name the 1961 Act, the 1986 minimum tariff, and the link to domestic violence (economic and emotional abuse for dowry is also PWDVA).

> **EXAM TIP:** Offence: giving, taking, abetting. 1986 Amendment teaching figure: minimum 5 years and fine Rs 15,000. Do not spend the short note on section 304B; that is the criminal-code companion.

[SN]Immoral Traffic Prevention Act[/SN]

IMMORAL TRAFFIC (PREVENTION) ACT, 1956
The Immoral Traffic (Prevention) Act, 1956 (ITPA), amended in 1986, is the principal Central statute against commercial sexual exploitation. It covers persons exploited sexually for commercial purposes, not only women. Offences include keeping a brothel, living on the earnings of prostitution, procuring, inducing, or taking a person for the sake of prostitution, and detaining a person in premises where prostitution is carried on.

The modern reading is that the Act targets trafficking, brothel-keeping, procuring, and living on earnings. It is not written as a statute whose primary object is to punish the person who is prostituted. Protective homes and corrective institutions are part of the scheme. Pair ITPA with the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 when the victim is under eighteen, and with the Immoral Traffic rules of the State for rescue and medical examination.

> **EXAM TIP:** 1956, Amendment 1986. Name brothel-keeping, living on earnings, and procuring. State that the Act covers all persons commercially exploited, not only women, and that punishment is aimed at trafficking and the brothel, not at the fact of being prostituted.

[SN]Commission of Sati Prevention Act[/SN]

COMMISSION OF SATI (PREVENTION) ACT, 1987
The Commission of Sati (Prevention) Act, 1987 prohibits the commission of sati and its glorification. Attempt to commit sati, abetment, and any ceremony, procession, or financial trust that glorifies sati are offences. Temples and memorials that glorify sati can be removed as provided. One paragraph is enough. The public-health point is that glorification is banned, not only the act itself.

> **EXAM TIP:** 1987. Ban on commission and on glorification. Stop there unless the examiner asks for abetment or temple powers.

[LAQ]Legislation for women's empowerment and maternal health[/LAQ]

LEGISLATION FOR WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND MATERNAL HEALTH
Definition. Women's empowerment and maternal-health legislation is the set of Central Acts that protect a woman's bodily autonomy, paid recovery after child-birth, safety in the shared household, freedom from dowry and from commercial sexual exploitation, and freedom from sex selection. Article 42 (maternity relief) and Article 15 (non-discrimination) are the constitutional pegs. Article 21 supplies emergency care and dignity.

LIMBS OF THE LONG ANSWER
1. Reproductive rights: Medical Termination of Pregnancy grounds (therapeutic, eugenic, humanitarian, failure of contraception for any woman and her partner, socio-economic environment) and the 2021 gestational table. Cross-refer clinical methods to family planning.
2. Stopping sex selection: PCPNDT registration, Form F, advertisement ban. This is a girl-child survival law as well as a women's law.
3. Maternal social security: Maternity Benefit Act twenty-six weeks, adopting and commissioning mothers twelve weeks, section 11A creche, work from home. One line: Employees' State Insurance maternity benefit is also twenty-six weeks (occupational-health leaf). The Code on Social Security, 2020 now maps the same field.
4. Violence and economic coercion: PWDVA as civil protection; Dowry Prohibition Act as a criminal prohibition of giving and taking; both feed the same household epidemic of injury and suicide.
5. Trafficking and sati: ITPA for commercial exploitation; Commission of Sati (Prevention) Act, 1987 for commission and glorification.

NATIONAL CONTEXT
These Acts convert Directive Principles into duties of employers, doctors, magistrates, and Appropriate Authorities. Programmes (Janani Suraksha Yojana, Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana, One Stop Centres) implement cash and services. The statute is what makes refusal of maternity leave, eviction from the shared household, a sex-selection advertisement, or a closed casualty for a raped woman a legal wrong.

CONCLUSION
Do not dump years. Open with Articles 42, 15, and 21. Draw the six-row table. Write two provisions under Medical Termination of Pregnancy, Maternity Benefit, and PWDVA, and one provision each under PCPNDT, Dowry, and ITPA. Close: empowerment statutes fail without Protection Officers, labour inspectors, and district Appropriate Authorities.

> **EXAM TIP:** Group, do not list. Six rows: MTP, PCPNDT, Maternity Benefit, PWDVA, Dowry, ITPA. Two provisions in the first three rows. One sentence on Employees' State Insurance 26 weeks and one sentence on the Social Security Code. End with enforcement.
