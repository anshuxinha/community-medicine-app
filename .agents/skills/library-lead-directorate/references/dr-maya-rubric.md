## 2. Core Review Responsibilities

### 2.1 The Three-Tier Knowledge Hierarchy
When auditing or synthesizing content, enforce this strict hierarchy of truth:

1. **Tier 1 (Official Guidelines, Statutes, & Real-Time National Statistics):**
   - *Sources:* MoHFW official guidelines, SRS (Sample Registration System) latest bulletins, NFHS (National Family Health Survey) reports, Central TB Division (NTEP), NCVBDC, NP-NCD guidelines, Indian Public Health Standards (IPHS 2022).
   - *Rule:* **Always takes absolute precedence.** Never revert a modern national statistic (e.g. SRS 2024 IMR 24 / MMR 97, NFHS-5 TFR 2.0, NP-NCD 2023-2030) to outdated figures published in older textbook editions.
2. **Tier 2 (Structured Modern Exam Topics from Golden Notes & Guidelines):**
   - *Sources:* `D:\Study Related\Books\Golden Notes Split` (high-yield topics such as Chandipura virus, KFD, Nipah virus, Tanahashi Model, Johari Window, CBR Matrix, Social Marketing, PM-ABHIM, PM-JANMAN, Kayakalp).
   - *Rule:* **Preserve without flattening.** Do not delete modern topics simply because they do not appear in Park. Never collapse structured comparison matrices, scoring tables, or step-by-step algorithms into generic prose paragraphs. Elevate their tone to textbook register while maintaining structured tables.
3. **Tier 3 (Park Classical Foundations):**
   - *Sources:* `D:\Study Related\Books\Park Split` (foundational epidemiology, screening concepts, biostatistics concepts, demography cycles, international health).
   - *Rule:* Synthesize the conceptual depth, historical evolution, and timeless principles of public health.

### 2.2 Dynamic, Intellectually Engaging Overviews
- **No Cookie-Cutter Formulas:** Overviews must NOT follow a repetitive template for every chapter.
- **Three Core Pillars of an Outstanding Overview:**
  1. *Philosophical Hook or Epigram:* Opening quote or fundamental human reality (e.g., Santayana on history, Alma-Ata on equity, Winslow on community effort, Noah's ark on planning).
  2. *Paradigmatic Evolution:* How medical science shifted (e.g., supernatural -> miasma -> germ theory -> epidemiological triad -> multicausal web; vertical disease eradication -> comprehensive primary health care).
  3. *Contemporary Indian & Global Significance:* Connecting principles to National Health Policy, Ayushman Bharat, Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 3), or current public health challenges.
- **Length:** 100 to 150 words of rich, inspiring prose.

### 2.3 Elimination of In-Text Textbook Attributions
- Public health facts and medical definitions should stand as objective medical knowledge.
- **Banned in Body Prose:**
  - `According to Park`, `Park outlines`, `Park defines`, `Park mentions`, `Park's classification`, `(Park)`.
- **Allowed:** Objective scientific references (e.g., `Under the WHO classification...`, `In the 1920 definition by C.E.A. Winslow...`, `As established by the Bhore Committee (1946)...`).
